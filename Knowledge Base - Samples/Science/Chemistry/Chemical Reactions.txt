Question: What is a chemical reaction?
Answer: A chemical reaction is a process in which one or more substances, called reactants, are transformed into one or more new substances, called products. During a chemical reaction, atoms are rearranged and chemical bonds may be broken or formed.

Question: What are reactants?
Answer: Reactants are the substances that participate in a chemical reaction and are transformed into products. In a chemical equation, reactants are conventionally written on the left side of the reaction arrow.

Question: What are products?
Answer: Products are the new substances formed as a result of a chemical reaction. In a chemical equation, products are conventionally written on the right side of the reaction arrow.

Question: What is a chemical equation?
Answer: A chemical equation is a symbolic representation of a chemical reaction using chemical formulas, coefficients, and symbols. It shows the reactants, products, and their relative amounts. A balanced chemical equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides.

Question: What is a combination reaction?
Answer: A combination reaction, also called a synthesis reaction, is a chemical reaction in which two or more substances combine to form a single product. A general form is A + B → AB. For example, 2H2 + O2 → 2H2O.

Question: What is a decomposition reaction?
Answer: A decomposition reaction is a chemical reaction in which a single compound breaks down into two or more simpler substances. A general form is AB → A + B. For example, CaCO3 → CaO + CO2 under suitable conditions.

Question: What is oxidation?
Answer: Oxidation is a chemical process involving the loss of electrons by a substance. In terms of oxygen, oxidation can also refer to the addition of oxygen to a substance. Oxidation and reduction occur together in redox reactions.

Question: What is reduction?
Answer: Reduction is a chemical process involving the gain of electrons by a substance. In terms of oxygen, reduction can also refer to the removal of oxygen from a substance. Reduction always occurs together with oxidation in a redox reaction.

Question: What is a catalyst?
Answer: A catalyst is a substance that increases the rate of a chemical reaction by providing an alternative reaction pathway with lower activation energy. A catalyst is not permanently consumed during the overall reaction and can be regenerated.

Question: What are signs of a chemical reaction?
Answer: Common observable signs of a chemical reaction include a change in color, formation of a gas, formation of a precipitate, release or absorption of heat, production of light, or a change in odor. These observations can indicate that new substances have formed, although some physical changes can produce similar observations.
