Question: What is a synonym?
Answer: A synonym is a word that has the same or a similar meaning as another word. Synonyms are not always interchangeable in every context because their meanings, tone, or usage may differ slightly.

Question: What is an antonym?
Answer: An antonym is a word that has an opposite or contrasting meaning to another word. Antonyms can express opposite qualities, states, actions, or concepts.

Question: What is a synonym for "happy"?
Answer: A common synonym for "happy" is "joyful." Other possible synonyms include cheerful, glad, delighted, and pleased, depending on the context.

Question: What is an antonym for "happy"?
Answer: A common antonym for "happy" is "sad." Other contrasting words may include unhappy, miserable, or sorrowful, depending on the context.

Question: What is a synonym for "big"?
Answer: A common synonym for "big" is "large." Other possible synonyms include huge, enormous, substantial, and gigantic, depending on the context.

Question: What is an antonym for "big"?
Answer: A common antonym for "big" is "small." Other possible contrasting words include little, tiny, and miniature, depending on the context.

Question: What is a synonym for "fast"?
Answer: A common synonym for "fast" is "quick." Other possible synonyms include rapid, speedy, swift, and prompt, depending on the context.

Question: What is an antonym for "fast"?
Answer: A common antonym for "fast" is "slow." The appropriate opposite can vary depending on whether "fast" refers to speed, movement, or another meaning.

Question: What is a synonym for "begin"?
Answer: A common synonym for "begin" is "start." Other possible synonyms include commence, initiate, launch, and originate, depending on the context.

Question: What is an antonym for "begin"?
Answer: A common antonym for "begin" is "end." Other possible contrasting words include finish, conclude, terminate, and stop, depending on the context.
