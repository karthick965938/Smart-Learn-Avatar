Question: What is modern history?
Answer: Modern history is the study of historical events, societies, ideas, and developments from the early modern period to the contemporary era. The exact starting point varies by historical tradition, but modern history commonly covers major political, economic, social, scientific, and technological transformations from around the 15th or 16th century onward.

Question: What was the Industrial Revolution?
Answer: The Industrial Revolution was a period of major economic, technological, and social transformation that began in Britain during the 18th century and later spread to other regions. It involved the development of mechanized production, factories, steam power, improved transportation, and new manufacturing processes. It contributed to rapid urbanization and major changes in work and society.

Question: What was the French Revolution?
Answer: The French Revolution was a major political and social upheaval in France that began in 1789 and led to the overthrow of the French monarchy, the establishment of a republic, and significant changes to French political and social institutions. The revolutionary period continued through the 1790s and had a lasting influence on ideas about citizenship, political rights, and government.

Question: What was colonialism?
Answer: Colonialism was a system in which a state established political control over another territory and its people, often through settlement, conquest, or other forms of domination. Colonial powers commonly controlled political institutions, land, labor, trade, and resources in the territories they governed.

Question: What was nationalism?
Answer: Nationalism is a political and cultural idea that emphasizes the identity, unity, interests, and self-determination of a nation or people. Nationalism has played different roles throughout history, including supporting movements for national independence as well as the formation and consolidation of nation-states.

Question: What was the First World War?
Answer: The First World War, also known as World War I, was a major international conflict fought primarily from 1914 to 1918. It involved major powers and alliances from Europe and other parts of the world. The war resulted in millions of military and civilian deaths and significantly changed the political boundaries and international order.

Question: What was the Second World War?
Answer: The Second World War, also known as World War II, was a global conflict fought primarily from 1939 to 1945. It involved many countries and major alliances, including the Allied and Axis powers. The war caused enormous loss of life and led to major political, economic, and geopolitical changes worldwide.

Question: What is independence?
Answer: Independence is the condition in which a country or political entity governs itself without being subject to the political authority of another country. Independence may be achieved through negotiation, political movements, conflict, or a combination of these processes.

Question: What was the Indian independence movement?
Answer: The Indian independence movement was a broad and long-running political and social movement seeking to end British colonial rule in the Indian subcontinent. It involved many organizations, leaders, movements, and forms of resistance, including mass civil disobedience and nonviolent campaigns. India became independent on 15 August 1947.

Question: Why is modern history important?
Answer: Modern history helps us understand major political, social, economic, scientific, and technological changes that shaped the contemporary world. It provides context for understanding modern nation-states, industrialization, colonialism, independence movements, global conflicts, international institutions, and current societies.
